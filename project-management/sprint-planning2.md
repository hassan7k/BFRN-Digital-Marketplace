### 4. Deliverables

By the end of Sprint 2, the group aims to have:

- An initial strategic BPMN model representing the BRFN enterprise.
- An analysis of major business processes and responsibilities within the organisation.
- Two candidate major functionalities identified to be agreed with group.
- An initial product backlog and sprint plan.
- A shared and organised project repository.
- Evidence of group activity and individual contributions.

### 5. Risks

- Functionality agreement: operational models and implementation
depend on the two major functionalities being agreed upon

- Model dependencies: operational BPMN models should be consistent
with the strategic BPMN and i* models.

- Camunda implementation: processes depend on the
operational workflows being correctly defined.

- Technical understanding: members may need time to become
familiar with Camunda Modeler, Camunda 8 Run, forms and process
execution.

- Integration: separately developed processes and components must
be reviewed and integrated.

- Group coordination: owners, deadlines and progress should be
agreed and reviewed regularly and outstanding work and blockers should
be raised early.

- Evidence and version control: work should be committed to GitHub
throughout the sprint, with clear commit messages and supporting
evidence.
